About this patcher

I made Kaizo Baconmon for my own runs, with a few extras you can use for Nuzlockes, Ironmon or your own rules. It takes plenty of inspiration from Ironmon VS. Ironmon, but tastier.

Leave Core Baconmon Package ticked for my full setup, or untick the bits you don’t want. If you’re playing a different challenge, check its rules first.

Designed for maximum compatibility between ROMs. Changes that cannot be applied are skipped and listed in the output log. I can’t guarantee compatibility with any particular ROM. Designed for clean US FireRed 1.1 (Rev 1) or Faster FireRed 1.3.2; other modified ROMs may also work.

What is Kaizo Baconmon?

Baconmon is my take on Kaizo Ironmon, with changes inspired by Ironmon VS and a few conveniences I wanted for my own runs. The full setup uses every package feature, a level 8 lab rival and your choice of Classic or Modern.

Ironmon was created by iateyourpie. The underlying rules are documented in the Ironmon rules reference. Credit to WaffleSmacker and the VS community for Ironmon VS, including early encounter rotation, the early balls and the scoring system. Baconmon is a separate variant: VS normally uses a level 6 lab rival; Baconmon defaults to 8.

Rules summary and key differences from Kaizo

Reach the Hall of Fame with one runner. Fainting ends the attempt. Use a fresh randomized seed after a loss; no reloading to undo outcomes. Use Set battles, follow the catch and pivot limits, and obey the item, move, ability and dungeon restrictions in the full rules below.

Pick a starter-ball position before seeing the choices and keep that position between runs. Baconmon allows that starter regardless of BST, and keeps the evolution exception. It does not allow over-limit wild pivots. Wild pivots still require committing before checking the caught Pokémon’s stats.

The added TM-paid relearner is allowed under Baconmon’s rules. Field HMs need no taught move, but still need their HM, badge and normal prerequisites. The lab machine can raise or lower friendship, Rock Tunnel is lit, and the forest nurse accepts poisoned parties. Optional dungeon healers remain banned. Guaranteed hidden-item refreshes do not permit farming.

Classic keeps Gen 3 battle mechanics. Modern adds the move-based physical/special split and updates existing abilities without adding abilities, learnsets or base stats. Choose the mode before the attempt. These are Baconmon exceptions, not changes to the official Kaizo rules.

Note: Fairy typing is intentionally omitted.

Modified ROM features and mode details
Core changes

I implemented the following changes from core Kaizo Ironmon:

- Start in Oak's lab after choosing your gender, name and rival's name. The opening rival battle still happens at level 8.

- Choose Classic or Modern at the start. Classic keeps Gen III battles. Modern adds the physical/special move split and updates existing abilities. It does not add abilities, learnsets or BST changes.

- Pick a starter ball before seeing the choices and keep that same position across runs. Your chosen starter is legal regardless of BST. This replaces normal starter selection. It does not allow over-limit wild pivots.

- Start with a Master Ball and five Poke Balls for legal catches.

- Early encounters rotate as they do in Ironmon VS.

- Use field HMs without teaching them or carrying an HM friend. You still need the HM, badge and normal story prerequisites. Fly from the Town Map.

- Rock Tunnel is already lit, so you do not need Flash to see there.

- Use the Move Reminder in the Move Deleter's Fuchsia house. Each successful relearn costs one TM of your choice. The usual Sketch restriction still applies.

- Use Oak's lab machine to maximise or plummet your runner's friendship. The former friendship helpers point you there.

- Use the Viridian Forest nurse even when poisoned. Dungeon beds and other optional dungeon healers remain banned.

- Chance-based hidden items are guaranteed to appear at their normal refresh. Collect each renewable location once; farming is still banned.

- Track progress with the 33-point VS-style score and dungeon-clear log. A dungeon bonus requires all its trainers in one visit without optional Center-equivalent healing.

- Press L while choosing an attack to read its description. Move summaries show physical, special and status symbols.

- A Baconmon title screen starring Grumpig.

- Start with Nuz-guide: the complete Baconmon rules, other challenge formats, mode details, scoring, credits and ROM information.

Everything else follows the full Kaizo Baconmon rules in this guide. Built-in conveniences do not waive the remaining restrictions.

Classic mode

Classic keeps FireRed's Gen III battle rules. Damaging moves use Attack or Special Attack according to their type, not according to the individual move.

Physical types are Normal, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Ground, Rock, Bug, Ghost and Steel. Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Psychic, Ice, Dragon and Dark are special.

A Dark move such as Bite is special in Classic. Ghost attacks such as Shadow Ball are physical. Status moves still use their own effects rather than a damage category.

Classic keeps the original ability effects. Sturdy blocks one-hit knockout moves; it does not protect a full-health Pokemon from an ordinary lethal hit.

You still get Baconmon's lab start, helpers, field HM access, light Rock Tunnel, TM-paid reminder, guide and other changes that are not battle-mode changes.

The seed's species, stat totals, randomized stat spread, types, abilities and learnsets remain that seed's data. Choosing Classic does not reroll them.

Modern: move split

In Modern, each damaging move has its own physical or special category. Bite is physical, Shadow Ball is special, and Hyper Beam is special. Status moves have a separate symbol.

The move's category chooses Attack and Defense, or Special Attack and Special Defense. A randomized move type does not change that move's modern category.

Reflect and Light Screen follow the move category. Counter, Mirror Coat, Hustle's physical accuracy check and the relevant damage handling use the split too.

Fire and Ice attacks can be physical. Weather, Flash Fire and Thick Fat follow the applicable move category.

Hidden Power and Weather Ball still work out their changing type through their normal effects, but remain special attacks in Modern. Their type change is not a switch to physical damage.

Move-category symbols are shown in the summary screen and the battle move-information panel. Press L while choosing a move to read the description. Use the D-pad to browse; L or B closes the panel.

Note: Fairy typing is intentionally omitted.

Modern abilities: A-F

Color Change waits until the final hit of a multi-hit move before changing the target's type.

Compound Eyes keeps its accuracy boost and improves wild held-item rolls. The common and rare slots use the modern 60% and 20% thresholds when the lead ability applies, instead of the usual 50% and 5% slots.

Drizzle starts five turns of rain. This does not shorten weather coming from a map or another permanent field effect.

Drought starts five turns of sunlight. Sand Stream uses the same five-turn rule for its sandstorm.

Effect Spore has a 30% total chance on eligible contact and does not affect a Grass-type attacker. The result can be sleep, poison or paralysis.

Static, Poison Point and Cute Charm use 30% contact chances instead of the original one-in-three checks. Their normal eligibility and immunity checks still apply.

Flash Fire responds to Fire moves, including status Fire moves, without the old frozen-user restriction. Its Fire damage bonus is applied to the appropriate move category.

Modern abilities: I-O

Illuminate has Keen Eye's battle protection here: it prevents accuracy drops and ignores a target's positive evasion stages. This does not make Rock Tunnel dark again.

Inner Focus prevents flinching and blocks Intimidate's Attack drop.

Keen Eye prevents accuracy drops and ignores positive evasion stages. Other accuracy modifiers still matter.

Lightning Rod redirects eligible Electric moves, including an ally's move in doubles. It absorbs Electric moves and raises Special Attack by one stage, up to the usual limit.

Oblivious blocks attraction, Taunt and Intimidate. Gaining the ability also clears an active Taunt here.

Own Tempo blocks confusion and Intimidate. It does not prevent unrelated Attack drops from other sources.

These are updates to abilities already present in Gen III. They do not add later abilities or change which ability a species has in the seed.

Modern abilities: P-Z

Pickup can recover an eligible berry or herb consumed earlier in the turn. The Pokemon needs an empty held-item slot.

Plus and Minus work with a living doubles partner that has either Plus or Minus. The partner does not have to have the opposite ability.

Rough Skin removes one eighth of the contact attacker's maximum HP, rather than the old one sixteenth.

Shadow Tag does not trap another Shadow Tag user. Ghost types also get the modern escape protection against trapping abilities.

Stench gives eligible damaging attacks a 10% flinch chance. It does not stack with the flinch-granting held-item effect handled by that routine.

Sturdy lets a full-health Pokemon survive an otherwise lethal direct hit with 1 HP. Later hits and residual damage can still finish it. It also works against confusion self-damage.

Synchronize reflects bad poison as bad poison instead of downgrading it to ordinary poison.

Trace avoids copying no ability, Trace or Forecast. It otherwise uses the native eligible-target logic.

Volt Absorb and Water Absorb can activate on the matching type's status moves as well as damaging moves. Thick Fat's Fire and Ice reduction follows the split.

What Modern does not change

Modern changes move categories and the ability effects listed here. Seed data stays unchanged: Pokemon, types, abilities, items, BSTs and learnsets.

Move power, accuracy, PP and secondary effects still come from the seed. Other mechanics keep Gen III rules.

The Huge Power and Pure Power restriction still applies. Check the move's category symbol.

Use the same mode when comparing runs.

Note: Fairy typing is intentionally omitted.

Full Kaizo Baconmon rule list

These are the full Baconmon rules, including the usual Kaizo restrictions and my changes. They assume the full package. If you change the options or rival level, agree on that before comparing runs.

Goal and run rules

Reach the Hall of Fame with one surviving runner. A fainted runner ends the run. Start the next attempt on a newly randomized seed. Do not reload to undo a loss, bad catch, move choice or other result.

The Full rules pages include the inherited Standard, Ultimate and Kaizo restrictions with the Baconmon changes applied. Read both rules menus, the battle mode pages and the point rules. You do not need to combine rules from the other challenge directories.

Agree on the same mode, starter-ball assignments and rules before a VS run. All restrictions still apply when the game or tracker allows an illegal action.

Seed and battle setup

Randomize Pokemon species, abilities, stat spreads, learnsets, trainer teams, wild encounters and items with the FireRed Kaizo preset. Use a new seed after each loss. Keep the same declared starter ball between runs.

Boost ordinary trainer and wild levels by 50%. Do not boost static encounter levels. Force trainer Pokemon to be fully evolved from level 30. Add three Pokemon to boss teams. Use Fluctuating growth for ordinary Pokemon and Slow growth for legendaries. Leave easier evolutions disabled. Randomize Pickup items with bad items excluded. Use the complete FireRed preset for the other randomizer options.

Choose Classic or Modern before the run and keep it for the whole attempt. Use Set battle style. Speed controls are allowed; undoing outcomes with save states is not.

You begin in Oak's lab after choosing your gender, name and rival name. You do not need to leave the lab to talk to Mom. The opening rival is level 8 in either mode. Fight the opening battle even if you dislike your starter.

You receive one Master Ball and five Poke Balls early. They are legal to use for catches that obey the catch rules. A Master Ball does not make an otherwise illegal pivot legal.

Starter and species limits

Choose left, middle or right before seeing any starter options. Keep that position across attempts. Do not inspect the choices and switch balls. In a VS event, agree on ball positions before anyone reveals the starters.

The Pokemon in your fixed ball is legal regardless of BST. This is only a BST exception: the move, ability and held-item restrictions still apply after the lab battle.

For wild pivots, 599 BST or higher is banned. Legendary and mythical Pokemon are banned except for at most one predeclared favourite, which must be below 600 BST. Declare up to three favourite species before starting; this list never overrides Baconmon's fixed starter ball. It does not remove the level, route or catch restrictions.

An evolution is allowed to take your legal runner above the BST limit or into a legendary species. You must let every evolution happen. Do not cancel it or use an Everstone. Catching an already over-limit wild Pokemon is not the same as evolving into it.

There is no starter reroll for a bad ability or moveset. Use the lab exceptions, then avoid prohibited actions or pivot. If your runner has no legal way to continue, the run is over.

Runner, catches and pivots

Use one Pokemon in battle. No backups, switching between runners, sacrifice Pokemon or extra party abilities. This ROM does not need HM friends. Do not keep extra Pokemon for Pickup, weather, item bonuses or any other advantage.

Obtain no more than six Pokemon during the run, counting your starter and accepted pivots. You may accept at most one Pokemon from each named route or area. It need not be the first encounter. Gifts and static encounters also use that area's allowance. Separate floors of one named dungeon do not create extra catches.

A catch may be at most four levels above your current runner. Commit to a pivot when you catch it, before examining its stats, moves or ability. Permanently retire the old runner; you cannot return to it later. Do not catch several candidates and compare them.

You may immediately box an unwanted catch without viewing any stats or moves and continue as if it had not been accepted. This is not permission to scout caught Pokemon or use a second battler. Throwing a ball to escape a dangerous wild encounter is allowed.

Do not deliberately knock out wild Pokemon for experience. Scout or run instead. A wild Pokemon destroying itself is not deliberate training. If you accidentally knock one out while scouting, you must pivot to a legal catch from that area before leaving or healing. If that cannot be done legally, the run cannot continue.

Shinies, nemesis and losses

You may defeat a wild shiny as a bonus knockout. Do not raise the shiny rate. This is not an extra catch allowance or permission to replace your fixed starter ball.

You may declare one nemesis species before playing. Once per run, you may deliberately defeat it in the wild. You may never catch it as a pivot. Evolving into it is allowed. Baconmon's fixed ball takes priority over ordinary Kaizo's nemesis-based starter selection.

Your runner fainting ends the attempt, including poison damage outside battle. Do not revive it, continue with a backup or recover its held item for another runner. Begin again on a newly randomized seed.

Save normally to stop playing and continue later. Save states may be used as a suspension tool only if they do not undo outcomes or reveal information you then act on in an earlier state. No resets for better damage rolls, encounters, catches or rewards.

Each trainer can be defeated once. No VS Seeker grinding. Collect each renewable item location once; do not farm respawning hidden items. Guaranteed hidden-item appearances change availability, not that restriction.

Moves, TMs and abilities

Use moves learned naturally by level or evolution, and compatible TMs handed to you directly for beating Gym Leaders. Ordinary found, bought or held TMs cannot be taught. General move tutors are banned.

Baconmon allows the Fuchsia Move Reminder in the Move Deleter's house. Pay one TM of your choice for each successfully remembered move. The payment TM's move is irrelevant. Cancelling or having no eligible move costs nothing. HMs cannot be payment. Do not relearn Sketch after that Pokemon has already used it to copy a move.

The following Gen III moves are banned in battle: Cut, Fly, Surf, Strength, Flash, Rock Smash, Waterfall and Dive; Spore and Assist; Absorb, Mega Drain, Giga Drain, Leech Life, Dream Eater, Leech Seed, Recover, Soft-Boiled, Rest, Milk Drink, Morning Sun, Synthesis, Moonlight, Wish, Slack Off, Ingrain, Swallow, Pain Split, Heal Bell, Aromatherapy and Refresh.

Do not use any other HP-restoring, draining or status-curing move if a modified seed includes one. Banned moves are still banned at full HP. Metronome may call one, and Mimic may temporarily copy and use one for that battle. Those exceptions do not let you permanently learn a banned move.

Huge Power and Pure Power prohibit physical attacks unless the Pokemon has 399 BST or less, or 400 to 410 BST inclusive and can still evolve. Special attacks remain legal. In Classic, category follows type; in Modern, use the move's physical or special icon.

The initial lab battle allows otherwise banned moves, abilities and held items. Once it ends, remove banned held items and stop using banned moves or prohibited physical attacks. Pivot if you cannot continue legally.

Items, shops and stealing

Only buy Poke Balls and Repels, including their different varieties. You may sell items. Do not buy healing items, vitamins, TMs, held items or other supplies. Do not ask an NPC to shop for you, or buy legal items to trade for prohibited supplies.

Use found or NPC-given items subject to the bans below. Lucky Egg and Sacred Ash are banned. Leftovers, Soul Dew and Everstone are banned. Focus Sash is banned if present. Do not use coloured flutes to cure status.

Allowed held items are items consumed when they activate, such as berries, Berry Juice and White Herb; items held solely for a required evolution; and Smoke Ball. Other permanent held items, including damage boosters and healing items, are banned. The initial lab battle is the held-item exception.

Do not intentionally steal wild held items with Thief, Covet or similar effects. If you accidentally take an item from a wild Pokemon you do not catch, discard it. Stealing from trainers is allowed, but you must remove a prohibited held item after the battle.

Using a found item does not waive the healing rules. The TM-paid Move Reminder is an express trade exception: you may spend an otherwise unusable TM there, but not buy TMs to supply him.

Healing and dungeon entries

Use HP, status and PP recovery items only during battle. Do not use them from the Bag outside battle. PP Up and PP Max are allowed outside battle. Found vitamins and evolution stones are not recovery items.

Pokemon Centers and equivalent healers are allowed outside dungeons. Baconmon also allows the Viridian Forest nurse, including when poisoned. Forests are not dungeons. The lab machine changes friendship only and does not restore HP, status or PP.

Enter a gym or dojo ready to defeat every trainer. You may not leave until all of them are beaten. No optional Center-equivalent healing inside.

Enter each other dungeon once unless the story requires another visit. Dungeons include caves, hideouts and buildings containing trainers. You may skip optional trainers in an ordinary dungeon, but skipped trainers mean no full-clear bonus. Leaving before completion does not grant permission to return for them.

Do not use dungeon healing stations, including the S.S. Anne bed, Silph bed or an NPC doing the same job as a Center. A forced story heal is allowed; returning voluntarily for another is not.

For a scored full clear, defeat all required trainer groups in one continuous visit without optional Center-equivalent healing. Floors, internal lifts and Silph warp tiles stay within that visit. Leaving, healing and returning forfeits the bonus and breaks the one-entry rule unless that return is story-required. Legal in-battle recovery items do not spoil a clean clear.

FireRed route and helper rules

On the S.S. Anne, fight the rival before any other ship trainer. On your first visit to Lavender Town, fight the Pokemon Tower rival before leaving town; return later with the Silph Scope for story progression. Do not travel to the Sevii Islands with Bill before beating the Elite Four.

The lab machine may set your runner's friendship to 255 or 0. Using it is allowed in Baconmon. The former friendship NPCs now point you to that machine and do not change friendship themselves.

Field HMs need their normal HM, badge and location or story prerequisites. Your runner need not learn the move, and no HM friend is required. Use the Town Map outdoors to Fly to an unlocked destination with HM02 and the Thunder Badge.

Cut needs Cascade; Fly needs Thunder; Surf needs Soul; Strength needs Rainbow; Flash needs Boulder; Rock Smash needs Marsh; Waterfall needs Volcano. Rock Tunnel is already lit. HMs are still banned as battle moves.

The tracker may record observed moves, abilities, notes, encounters, ratings, cards and progress. These are aids, not extra catches or battle permissions. Review unrevealed seed details only after your run ends; do not consult the randomizer's spoiler log during an active attempt.

The challenge ends when you beat the Champion and enter the Hall of Fame. A score record does not excuse a rule breach. All the rules above still apply if the game or tracker lets you perform an illegal action.

Baconmon helpers

The lab machine changes only your lead Pokemon's friendship. Maximise sets it to 255. Plummet sets it to 0. Cancel changes nothing. It is not a healing machine.

The lab aide and both Viridian friendship helpers now direct you to the lab machine. Talking to them does not change friendship.

The Viridian Forest nurse restores the party and clears poison. You can use her outside a dungeon attempt. Leaving Mt. Moon to visit her and then returning does not count as a clean Mt. Moon clear.

The Move Reminder sits opposite the Move Deleter in Fuchsia. Pick a forgotten move from the list. Your payment TM is his fee; the move on it doesn't matter.

Choose the TM you are willing to lose. It is removed only after a move is successfully remembered. Cancelling, choosing an Egg or having no eligible moves costs nothing. HMs are not payment.

Paying with an otherwise unusable TM is allowed in Baconmon. Teaching an ordinary found or bought TM is still restricted by Kaizo rules. Do not use the reminder to repeat Sketch after it has copied a move.

HMs and travel

Keep the required HM in your Bag and earn the badge that unlocks it. Interact with the obstacle as normal. Your runner does not need to know the move.

Cut needs the Cascade Badge. Fly needs the Thunder Badge. Surf needs the Soul Badge. Strength needs the Rainbow Badge. Flash needs the Boulder Badge. Rock Smash needs the Marsh Badge. Waterfall needs the Volcano Badge.

Other normal checks still matter: have a Pokemon, use the move in a suitable place and meet story or location requirements. This is not permission to skip progression.

Use the Town Map outdoors to choose an unlocked Fly destination. It works from the Bag or as a registered item. You still need HM02 and the Thunder Badge.

Rock Tunnel's two floors are lit. You do not need Flash to see there. That does not turn every other HM or badge check off.

HMs remain banned as battle moves under the inherited Ultimate rules. Removing the need to teach one does not make Surf, Strength or Fly a legal attack for Kaizo.

Sources: Standard, Ultimate and Kaizo Ironmon; Ironmon VS. These describe their own formats; the Baconmon exceptions above belong to this project.

Nuz-guide key item and point tracking

I included the VS-style points because I find tracking progress and chasing a PB more fun than only counting a Champion win. Kaizo Ironmon can take dozens, hundreds or even thousands of attempts. Points give you smaller milestones to aim for, a way to see yourself improving, and something to compete over with friends—even when nobody wins the seed.

The Nuz-guide option adds a key item given at the start. It contains challenge directories, the full Baconmon rules, mode details, credits and a score log. A selects and turns pages; B goes back. It explains the full setup and tracks progress. You still need to follow the rules, including any changes you’ve made to the options.

The VS-style total is 33: Brock 2; each other Gym Leader 1; each Elite Four member 2; Champion 5. Clean dungeon bonuses are Mt. Moon 1, S.S. Anne 1, Rock Tunnel 1, Silph Co. 3 and Victory Road 5.

A dungeon bonus needs every required trainer in one uninterrupted visit, without a Pokémon Center or equivalent heal. Leaving unfinished, healing and returning does not count. Silph’s bed and the S.S. Anne healer are not permitted. The ROM monitors visits, trainer flags and full heals, but it cannot prove every rule was followed.

The ROM keeps the current run’s score and clean-clear log. Keep your PB separately so you can compare future runs. It does not connect to the official VS competition service.

How to randomise: Android or PC

For a new run, patch a fresh, unrandomised US FireRed 1.1 copy first. Keep that as your base for new seeds. Don’t randomise a run you’re already playing. You can make new seeds with the batch randomiser beta below, or use the PC and Android tools linked here.

Pick settings for your challenge

A normal Nuzlocke does not require randomization. If you want one, choose your own encounter and trainer settings and agree on clauses first. For Standard, Ultimate or Kaizo Ironmon, use the matching official FireRed settings, with a compatible randomizer version. Do not use a NatDex or another game’s preset for this ROM.

For Baconmon, start with the FireRed Kaizo settings and follow the Seed and battle setup page in the full rules above. Keep the prescribed trainer teams, level boosts, growth rates and forced evolutions; Modern mode does not set them for you. With the default rival adjustment selected, the opening rival stays at level 8 during play. If you choose a different rival level, patch the finished randomized seed with your chosen value before playing, and verify the battle level. Later randomization can change a custom trainer level.

Keep the same settings and Classic/Modern choice when comparing seeds. Keep the spoiler log for after the run; do not read it during a blind attempt. Patch notes list deliberate changes and anything skipped.

On PC
  1. Get Universal Pokémon Randomizer ZX. Extract the download and use its launcher. Follow its Java instructions if needed.
  2. Open your patched, unrandomized FireRed copy. Import the matching settings string or .rnqs preset from the official settings reference.
  3. Check the game and challenge, then randomize to a new filename. Batch generation can produce a set of fresh seeds. The Ironmon Tracker setup guide explains its PC automation.
  4. If using a custom rival level, apply that option to the generated seed before playing. Read its notes. Test the opening on a separate save.
  5. For Android play, transfer the generated .gba files to your phone and select their folder in the emulator. Keep the original files and your battery saves.
On Android, without a PC
  1. Get UPR-Android from champred’s releases. Its usage instructions cover opening ROMs and importing settings.
  2. Open your patched, unrandomized FireRed copy. Paste a compatible FireRed settings string or choose the appropriate preset, then check it against your chosen rules. Use the ordinary FireRed format, not NatDex/MAX support.
  3. Generate a new ROM, or a batch of numbered ROMs. Save them in a folder you can open in your chosen GBA emulator. Start from a fresh save for each seed.
  4. Batch mode is the simpler fallback. IronMon Emu also supports UPR mode through compatible UPR-Android builds: load the base and settings in the randomizer, then long-press the ROM family in the emulator to select UPR mode. See the upstream setup guide. Follow the emulator’s own instructions for automatic rerolling.

If a settings string is rejected, check the randomizer version instead of silently choosing different settings. Custom rival levels need checking after randomization; use batch mode and patch the completed seeds when required.
