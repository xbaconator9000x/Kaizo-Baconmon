# A note on reuse

If you use my Baconmon changes in your own project, I'd love to see what you make! Send me a link through a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/Kaizo-Baconmon/issues) or a message. You don't need to notify me or wait for approval.

I want this project to be something people can enjoy and build on. Please don't use my work to degrade, discriminate against or intentionally harm anyone. Keep it welcoming.

These are personal requests, not additional licence conditions. The [reuse licence](LICENSE.txt) sets out the non-commercial and credit requirements for my original ROM changes. The patcher software and other creators' work keep their own terms.
